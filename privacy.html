Privacy policy

How KakobuyQC handles information when you browse this site. Kakobuy and other linked services have their own privacy policies.

What this site is

KakobuyQC is a product discovery and QC reference site. You do not create an account here, and this site does not take payments or maintain a customer account database.

Information stored in your browser

Your wishlist and product reviews are saved in local browser storage on the device where you add them. They are not sent to a KakobuyQC account or uploaded by this site. You can remove them by clearing this site's storage in your browser settings.

Service worker and app installation

If your browser supports it, KakobuyQC can be installed as a web app. A service worker caches core pages, scripts, and icons to help the site reopen. Wishlist and review data remain in your browser storage.

Cookies, analytics, and advertising

The current site code does not include a separate analytics or advertising tracking service. Your browser and the hosting provider may process ordinary technical request information, such as IP address, browser type, and requested page, as part of delivering and protecting the site.

Fonts and third-party links

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts. When you open a Kakobuy, Weidian, Taobao, 1688, or other external link, that provider receives your request and may collect information under its own policy. Kakobuy product links may contain an affiliate code so a referral can be attributed to KakobuyQC.

Retention and your choices

Wishlist and review entries remain in local browser storage until you remove them or clear site data. You can avoid third-party font requests by using browser controls that block external resources, though the page may then use fallback fonts.
